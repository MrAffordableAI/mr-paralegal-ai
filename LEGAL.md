- a lawyer, paralegal employed by a law firm, or law practice

Authorities that cannot be retrieved and verified are marked unverified. A fluent write-up is not the same as verified law.

Unauthorized practice of law rules differ by state. Do not market this app as a lawyer or as guaranteed legal advice. Have counsel review any public-facing claims before you charge for it or ship it to consumers.
